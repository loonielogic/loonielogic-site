## Educational content only

Everything on LoonieLogic (explainers, calculators, comparisons, and verdicts) is general financial education for Canadians. It is not financial advice, tax advice, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product. Always do your own research or talk to a licensed professional before making financial decisions.

## Estimates, not promises

Our calculators produce estimates from the assumptions shown on each page. Real outcomes depend on factors we can't see: your full financial picture, future rule changes, and market returns. Treat every number here as a starting point for your own math, not a guarantee.

## Accuracy and updates

We verify figures against official sources (CRA, Government of Canada, regulators) and stamp every page with its last-reviewed date. Rules change, so check the linked official pages before acting on anything time-sensitive, like contribution deadlines.
