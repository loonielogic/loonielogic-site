## How LoonieLogic makes money

LoonieLogic is free to read, and we plan to keep it that way. We earn money through **affiliate commissions**: some links on this site point to financial products (brokerages, banks, tax software), and if you sign up through those links, the company may pay us a commission. It costs you nothing extra.

## Where you'll see it disclosed

- Every comparison page carries a short disclosure bar above the fold, before the first affiliate link.
- Every affiliate link goes through our own `/go/` address and carries `rel="sponsored nofollow"`.
- Pages with no live affiliate relationships say so explicitly, instead of disclosing commissions they can't earn.

## What affiliate money doesn't buy

Our verdicts are never for sale. We rank products by what's best for the reader (fees, features, and fit), and the commission a product pays doesn't move it up the list. If a product that pays us loses to one that doesn't, the better product still wins, and we'll say so in plain language.

## Current status

As of the last-reviewed date above, our affiliate applications are still pending. The `/go/` links on this site don't go anywhere yet, and we earn nothing from them. This page will be updated the moment that changes, because the disclosure has to match reality.

## The legal bit

This disclosure follows the Competition Act's requirements for testimonials and endorsements: any material connection between us and a product we recommend must be clearly disclosed where the recommendation appears. That's what this page, and the disclosure bars across the site, are for.
