## What LoonieLogic is

LoonieLogic is a Canadian financial education site built on a simple funnel: **Learn** the rules in plain English, **Try** them with calculators that do real Canadian math, and **Do** something with the comparison pages that actually recommend.

Most Canadian finance content describes your options and leaves you to guess. We say which one to pick, and show the math behind the pick.

## What LoonieLogic is not

We are not financial advisors, and nothing on this site is financial advice or a substitute for a licensed professional. Our calculators produce estimates from the assumptions shown, not recommendations. Tax rules, limits, and promotions change. The verification dates on every page tell you how fresh the numbers are.

## How we make money

Through affiliate commissions: when you sign up for a product through certain links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every page that carries affiliate links discloses it before the first link, and our full [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure) lists how it works. Recommendations are never sold: if a product pays us and a competitor doesn't, the better product still wins.

## Why the name

The loonie is the most Canadian coin there is. Logic is what your money decisions deserve. Put them together and you get the whole mission: Canadian money, explained clearly enough to act on.
