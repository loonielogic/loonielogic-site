## The short version

We collect almost nothing. This is a static website: there are no accounts and no tracking cookies. We run privacy-friendly analytics (Umami Cloud, plus Cloudflare Web Analytics for page-view counts and load performance) that counts page views and broad usage patterns without identifying you. Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and never sent to us.

## What we do collect

- **Server logs:** our hosting provider may log standard technical data (IP address, pages requested, timestamps) to keep the site running and secure. We don't use these logs to identify you.
- **Affiliate redirects:** when you click a product link, the redirect passes through our `/go/` address so we can count clicks in aggregate. We don't attach your identity to it, and we never see your information on the merchant's site.
- **Website analytics:** we use Umami Cloud and Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the site is used. Both set no cookies, do not fingerprint your device, and do not record your name, email, or any financial details. See "Website analytics" below for exactly what is recorded.
- **Newsletter signups (optional):** if you choose to subscribe, your email address (and first name, if you give one) goes directly to our email provider, not to this website. See "Email newsletter" below.

## What we never collect

We never ask for financial details. The only place we ask for your email or name is the optional newsletter signup. Our calculators run entirely in your browser. The numbers you type never leave your device.

## Website analytics

We use Umami Cloud (hosted analytics, privacy-focused) to learn which pages and tools people use, so we can improve them. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and measures real page-load performance; it cannot see anything you do inside the calculators. There is no cookie banner because there are no cookies to consent to.

What is recorded:
- **Page views:** which pages are visited, counted in aggregate.
- **Usage events:** a small set of named events, for example which guide path was clicked on the homepage, which hub card was opened, or whether the planner's results were viewed. Event details are limited to broad classes and bands (for example a province code like "on", or a savings-rate band). Raw numbers you type into calculators, income, debt, home prices, and anything that could identify you are never sent.

What is not recorded: no cookies, no device fingerprinting, no cross-site tracking, no advertising identifiers, and no attempt to work around content blockers. If your blocker stops the analytics script, the site works exactly the same.

**Opting out:** because there are no cookies, there is nothing to delete. If you prefer not to be counted at all, block the analytics scripts (served from cloud.umami.is and static.cloudflareinsights.com) with a content blocker.

## Email newsletter

Signup is not open yet. This section describes how it will work when it opens, and it will be updated with the provider's name before the first address is collected.

**Why we ask:** we use your email only to send the LoonieLogic newsletter (new guides, tool updates, and Canadian money deadlines, about twice a month). It is never sold or shared, and it is never used for advertising.

**Who handles it:** the signup form sends your address straight to the email service provider that delivers the newsletter. This website never sees, stores, or logs it. We have not chosen a provider yet, so we cannot yet tell you which company that is or which country it stores data in. We will name the provider here, with where it stores data and which companies it relies on, before the signup opens.

**Double opt-in:** after you subscribe, you get one email with a confirm link that expires in 48 hours. Nothing else is sent until you click it. If you don't click it, you are not subscribed and you will not get the newsletter.

**Consent records:** to meet Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), the provider keeps a record of when you subscribed, where on the site, and the exact wording you agreed to. We keep these records for 3 years after your consent ends, then delete them.

**Worksheets and other downloads:** if you request a free worksheet, we use your email to send you that worksheet. It does not sign you up for the newsletter. That takes a separate box, which starts unticked.

**Identification and unsubscribing:** every email identifies LoonieLogic, with our mailing address and a way to contact us, and carries an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing takes one click, with no login and no questions. We process it the same day (the law allows up to 10 business days). After you unsubscribe, you get no further newsletters unless you sign up again.

**Measurement:** our site analytics count a signup form submission and a confirmation as anonymous events, labelled only with where on the site the form was (for example "homepage"). Your address and name are never part of them. Open and click counts for the emails themselves stay with the email provider.

## Your rights (PIPEDA)

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Given how little we collect, there is usually nothing to access, but the right still applies, and you can exercise it by contacting us through the site.
