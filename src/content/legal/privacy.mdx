## The short version

We collect almost nothing. This is a static website: there are no accounts and no tracking cookies. We run privacy-friendly analytics (Umami Cloud) that counts page views and broad usage patterns without identifying you. Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and never sent to us.

## What we do collect

- **Server logs:** our hosting provider may log standard technical data (IP address, pages requested, timestamps) to keep the site running and secure. We don't use these logs to identify you.
- **Affiliate redirects:** when you click a product link, the redirect passes through our `/go/` address so we can count clicks in aggregate. We don't attach your identity to it, and we never see your information on the merchant's site.
- **Website analytics:** we use Umami Cloud to understand how the site is used. Umami sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your device, and does not record your name, email, or any financial details. See "Website analytics" below for exactly what is recorded.

## What we never collect

We don't ask for your name, email, or financial details. Our calculators run entirely in your browser. The numbers you type never leave your device.

## Website analytics

We use Umami Cloud (hosted analytics, privacy-focused) to learn which pages and tools people use, so we can improve them. There is no cookie banner because there are no cookies to consent to.

What is recorded:
- **Page views:** which pages are visited, counted in aggregate.
- **Usage events:** a small set of named events, for example which guide path was clicked on the homepage, which hub card was opened, or whether the planner's results were viewed. Event details are limited to broad classes and bands (for example a province code like "on", or a savings-rate band). Raw numbers you type into calculators, income, debt, home prices, and anything that could identify you are never sent.

What is not recorded: no cookies, no device fingerprinting, no cross-site tracking, no advertising identifiers, and no attempt to work around content blockers. If your blocker stops the analytics script, the site works exactly the same.

**Opting out:** because there are no cookies, there is nothing to delete. If you prefer not to be counted at all, block the analytics script (served from cloud.umami.is) with a content blocker.

## Your rights (PIPEDA)

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Given how little we collect, there is usually nothing to access, but the right still applies, and you can exercise it by contacting us through the site.
